# Third-party notices

## Reeds–Shepp algorithm references

`planner/reeds_shepp.py` implements the formulas published by James A. Reeds III and
Lawrence A. Shepp in *Optimal paths for a car that goes both forwards and backwards*
(Pacific Journal of Mathematics, 1990). Its implementation was cross-checked against:

- Open Motion Planning Library (OMPL), `ReedsSheppStateSpace.cpp`, Copyright (c) 2010
  Rice University, BSD 3-Clause License.
- PythonRobotics, `reeds_shepp_path_planning.py`, Copyright (c) 2016 Atsushi Sakai and
  contributors, MIT License.

Those projects' license texts and current sources are available from their upstream
repositories. No runtime dependency on either project is introduced here.
